# Nashordaq

Nashordaq is a private virtual market for a League of Legends group.

## Language

**Private market**:
A group's shared game environment with tradable players, portfolios and a leaderboard.
_Avoid_: Public exchange, global market

**User**:
A participant with a Nashordaq account and personal game state.
_Avoid_: Riot account, tracked player

**Tracked player**:
A League of Legends account whose ranked progression determines the performance of its tradable shares.
_Avoid_: User, login account

**Demo user**:
A temporary demo participant with personal game state in the shared simulated market.
_Avoid_: Shared demo account

**Streamer alias**:
A permanently assigned League of Legends name that replaces a user's identity in Streamer Mode. Champion names form the initial pool; item and ability names extend it. Unlinked tracked players also have a permanent alias.
_Avoid_: Username, RiotName
